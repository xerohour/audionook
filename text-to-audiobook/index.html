Text to Audiobook Generator

This tool can generate very long audiobooks. It does this by chunking your text, generating audio for those chunks, and then joining those clips once it is finished. It was made using kokoro-js, which means it runs in your browser (i.e. the text is not sent to a server). Since it runs on your device, the speed at which it processes the chunks depends on how powerful your device is. You may want to leave it running and go do something else for a few hours. But you should keep this browser tab visible/foregrounded (e.g. by pulling the tab out into its own window) to ensure your browser doesn't 'throttle' its compute usage, which it does for 'backgrounded' tabs after a while.

You can use the audio you generate for any purpose - commercial use is allowed!

Tip: If you've got messy text (e.g. formatting issues due to copy-paste from a PDF) then you can use this AI-powered text cleaner to automatically tidy it up before pasting it below.
